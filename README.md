# Growth Unhinged Skills

A Claude Cowork plugin with 17 GTM skills built from 5 years of writing [Growth Unhinged](https://www.growthunhinged.com) — covering pricing, newsletter growth, ICP, SEO, content strategy, and more.

## Install

Download [`growth-unhinged-skills.plugin`](./growth-unhinged-skills.plugin) and open it in Claude Cowork.

## Skills included

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| **ai-brain-for-positioning** | Build one AI project that holds your customer evidence and positioning, then rewrite every touchpoint from it |
| **ai-pricing-visibility-audit** | Score how ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot, and Claude see and cite your pricing, out of 100 |
| **b2b-newsletter-growth** | 25-tactic playbook for growing a B2B newsletter, from launch to scale |
| **content-editor** | Pre-publish editorial review with fact-checking, SEO recommendations, and tracked changes |
| **content-performance-analyzer** | Benchmarks your newsletter archive to find what's resonating and what's underperforming |
| **deep-research-gtm** | In-depth research reports for competitive analysis, market sizing, and GTM strategy |
| **gtm-plays-brainstorm** | Brainstorms and prioritizes automated outbound and signal-based GTM plays |
| **icp-sharpener** | Sharpens your ICP into 2–3 specific, actionable segments with firmographic signals and messaging hooks |
| **linkedin-punch-up** | Edits and improves LinkedIn post drafts before publishing |
| **pricing-metric-selector** | Identify the right unit to charge for — seats, usage, outcomes, and more |
| **pricing-quick-win** | Diagnoses your pricing model and delivers near-term action items to extract more revenue |
| **pricing-teardown** | Grades your pricing page across 10 dimensions with specific, prioritized recommendations |
| **seo-checker** | SEO audit for newsletters and blogs, plus meta description writing for individual posts |
| **storytelling-archetype** | Identifies the right content archetype for your brand and recommends formats, channels, and ideas |
| **subscriber-health-audit** | Audits your newsletter list health, segments by engagement tier, and delivers a re-engagement plan |
| **win-loss-pricing-analyzer** | Code sales calls and CRM notes into win/loss themes and size the revenue won or lost on pricing |
| **wtp-survey-builder** | Build a value and willingness-to-pay survey ready to field in Typeform, SurveyMonkey, Qualtrics, or Google Forms |

## Optional: connect beehiiv

Several skills (content-editor, content-performance-analyzer, seo-checker, subscriber-health-audit) get richer results when beehiiv is connected via MCP — they pull live post data and subscriber stats automatically. All skills work without it using pasted data or URLs.
